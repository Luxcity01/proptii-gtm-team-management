    Why we are doing this

    The problem, on both sides

    For landlords

    • Letting one property means a spreadsheet, a listing site, a referencing provider and a contract template, none of them connected.
    • Referencing and contracts are the slowest steps, and the ones where a mistake costs most.
    • When something goes wrong, the records are in four places.

    For us

    • We’ve built viewings, referencing and contracts in one place. We don’t yet have landlords using it week to week.
    • We don’t know which route brings landlords who stay.
    • Our planning is ahead of our customer evidence. This month is about closing that gap.
    Focus

    One customer, one area

    Who and where

    • Self-managing landlords with 1 to 10 properties, who handle letting, referencing and contracts themselves.
    • South East London and North Kent, where we already hold property and know the market.
    • Reached through buy-to-let brokers, landlord accountants, local landlord groups and introductions from other landlords.

    Why not agents first

    • Agents decide slowly, already run established software, and switching costs them more.
    • Landlords decide in one call, switch cheaply, and feel the pain of referencing and contracts directly.
    • We can’t cold email individual landlords, so partners become our main channel. Agents come back into view in month 2.

    The objective

    One customer, one area, one number.

    By the end of day 30, twenty self-managing landlords in South East London and North Kent have created an account, added at least one property, and sent at least one invite.

    That sits at rung 3 of the traction ladder on the GTM essentials page. It is the target. The rungs above it are the evidence. Five landlords who come back unprompted would say more about the product than twenty who stopped at rung 3.

    Why landlords rather than agents

    Independent agentsSelf-managing landlords
    Who decidesBranch owner, usually slowlyThe landlord, often in one call
    What they use nowEstablished agency softwareSpreadsheets, listing sites, email
    Cost of switchingHighLow
    Cold emailAllowed for limited companies, with opt-outNeeds consent, as they are individuals
    Best way inDirect outreachBrokers, accountants, landlord groups, referrals

    Landlords decide quickly, switch cheaply, and referencing and contracts are exactly where it hurts for them. One area means referrals can build on each other, and you already hold property and know the market there.

    You cannot cold email individual landlords, which pushes the plan towards partners. A buy-to-let broker’s client list is full of your target customers. In that direction the broker sends landlords to you, which should carry much lighter regulatory weight than Proptii routing borrowers to brokers. Confirm that with whoever advises you on FCA questions before relying on it.

    Call guide

    Questions that get honest answers. Ask about what people have done, not what they say they would do.

    Landlord discovery

    1. Talk me through the last time you let a property. What took the longest?
    2. How do you handle referencing today, and what does it cost you?
    3. Where do your tenancy agreements live right now?
    4. Tell me about the last tenancy that started badly. What happened?
    5. What have you tried to make this easier? Why did you stop using it?
    6. How many properties do you have, and how often do they change tenants?
    7. Who else do you know who lets their own properties?
    Avoid “Would you use this?” and “Do you like the idea?” People say yes to be polite, and the answer tells you nothing.

    Partner call

    1. How many of your clients manage their own properties?
    2. What do they complain to you about, apart from finance?
    3. What would you need to see before recommending a tool to them?
    4. How would you like to hear back about people you introduce?

    Onboarding call

    1. Ask permission to record the call.
    2. Add their property first, live, on their screen.
    3. Book or log one real viewing.
    4. Send the first invite while you are still on the call.
    5. Ask what nearly stopped them.
    6. Agree the date of the next check-in.
