# Messaging

**Owners:** PM and UI/UX. **Agreed:** [date]

The words everyone uses. Each section below goes into the matching box in the Messaging section of the Essentials page. Change the words there first, then everywhere else.

## Who we are for

Self-managing landlords with 1 to 10 rental properties in South East London and North Kent, who find, reference and sign their own tenants, and today juggle a spreadsheet, a listing site, a referencing provider and a contract template to do it.

## Who we are not for, yet

- Letting and managing agents.
- Landlords whose properties are fully managed by an agent.
- Landlords with more than 10 properties.
- Landlords whose properties are all outside South East London and North Kent.
- Tenants looking for a home. Tenants join Proptii when their landlord invites them.

## One line for landlords

> Viewings, referencing and contracts for your rental, in one place, instead of four tools that don't talk to each other.

Alternative to test: "Let your next property without the spreadsheet, the referencing website and the contract template."

Replace either line with a landlord's own words as soon as the language bank has a better one.

## One line for partners (brokers and landlord accountants)

> Your landlord clients lose time and money between tenancies on referencing and paperwork. Proptii puts viewings, referencing and contracts in one place, and we tell you how every client you introduce is getting on.

Check with the FCA adviser before using this with brokers (risk r10 on the Plan page).

## One line for tenants (invite email)

> [Landlord name] has invited you to [book a viewing / complete your referencing / review your tenancy agreement] on Proptii. It takes a few minutes.

Only add "You don't need an account to start" once that works in the product (week 1 task w1i).

## Proof we can stand behind

What we can show today:

- **The full journey works in one place.** A landlord can add a property, book a viewing, reference a tenant and send a contract. We show it live, on their screen. Only claim this once the week 0 journey test (w0a) has passed and referencing runs through a live provider (w0c).
- **We are local.** We hold property in South East London and North Kent ourselves, so we know the market.
- **The design partner terms are real.** Six months free and a direct line to the team, in writing on the design partner one-pager.

Add these as they arrive, and only with evidence:

- Design partner quotes, with written permission and the person's name or initials agreed.
- Measured time savings, such as working days to first property or first invite, taken from the dashboard and not estimated.
- A count of landlords using Proptii, checked against the dashboard on the day it's posted.
- The first case study (week 3), once the design partner has cleared it.

Never use:

- The 41% and £2,700 figures from the early pitch, until they're sourced.
- Any saving, percentage or customer count we haven't measured ourselves.
