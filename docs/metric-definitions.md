# Metric definitions

**Agreed:** [date], by CEO, Head of Eng, PM, UI/UX

These apply from Monday 16 November. We don't change them mid-month. Changes are made at the week 4 review, with the date written down.

## Objective

20 self-managing landlords in South East London and North Kent, by Friday 11 December, who have created an account, added a property and sent an invite.

- [Decide: does the invite have to be to a tenant, or does introducing another landlord also count?]
- This is stricter than "Activated" (rung 4). We report both numbers.

## Who counts

- **Self-managing landlord:** owns 1 to 10 rental properties and handles letting, referencing and contracts without a managing agent. Confirmed on the call or at sign-up.
- **In the area:** at least one property in [SE, BR, DA, ME] postcodes.
- **Excluded:** team accounts, test accounts, and friends and family who aren't real landlords.
- **Design partner:** one of our first five landlords, on the terms on the Essentials page. Counted in every number like any other landlord.

## Channel

- Each landlord's channel is the link code on their first sign-up.
- No link code: we use what they tell us on the call, marked "self-reported".
- Each landlord has one channel. It doesn't change later.

## Funnel (the dashboard columns)

- **Contacted:** we sent a message to a named person or group (email, DM, post or introduction). One per person, per channel.
- **Replied:** any reply from a person, including a no. Auto-replies don't count.
- **Conversation:** a live call or meeting of at least 10 minutes with a landlord or partner. Message threads don't count.
- **Landlord:** created an account.

## Traction ladder

Each landlord sits at the highest rung they've reached.

1. **Outreached:** we contacted them by message, introduction or a reply to a post. Same rule as "Contacted".
2. **Interested:** a call booked through the booking link, or agreed in writing.
3. **Committed:** said yes to onboarding with a date, or became a design partner.
4. **Activated:** at least one property saved with an address.
5. **Got value:** completed a viewing, reference or contract end to end with a real tenant. Test emails don't count.
6. **Came back:** did something meaningful on another day, at least 48 hours after their first session, with no prompt from us in the 48 hours before.
7. **Brought someone:** sent an invite to a tenant, or introduced another landlord without being asked.
8. **Would pay:** named a specific price in writing or on a recorded call, or paid.

**Meaningful action** = adding a property, sending an invite, or moving a viewing, reference or contract forward. Logging in alone doesn't count.

## Process

- **Stalled:** an account with no meaningful action for 3 working days. The CEO gets an alert.
- **Reporting week:** Monday 00:00 to Friday 17:00, UK time. Numbers are frozen at 17:00 for the Friday gate.
- **Where each number comes from:** product numbers from [the database], contacted, replied and conversations from [the CRM or comms log]. Nobody counts from memory.
- **Corrections:** counting mistakes are fixed and noted on the day they're found. We don't change past gate decisions.
